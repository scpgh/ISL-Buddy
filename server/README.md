- **Linguistic & Theoretical Guidelines:**
  Manners in ISL rely on pleasant, clear non-manual facial markers. Respectful terms like 'Please' and 'Thank You' are signed with gentle palm movements centered near the chest box. Establishing friendly eye contact is the absolute foundation of Deaf communication etiquette.
- **Detailed Practice Steps:**
  - Step 1: Face your camera or mirror with a calm, smiling expression.
  - Step 2: Sign 'Please' by placing your dominant palm on your chest and moving it in a polite circular direction.
  - Step 3: Practice 'Excuse me' by gently brushing your active hand over your base hand to show respectful intent.
- **Covered Vocabulary:** Please, Thank you, Excuse me, Sorry, Welcome
- **🎮 Duolingo Interactive Challenges:**
  1. **Multiple Choice Question:**
     * *Prompt:* What is the primary role of facial expressions when signing 'Thank You' or 'Please' in ISL?

     * ❌ They must always remain completely deadpan and static
     * ❌ They replace the hand movements entirely
  2. **Vocabulary Matching Pairs:**
     * `Please` ↔ `Palm circle on chest`
     * `Sorry` ↔ `Fist rubbing chest`
  3. **Sentence Builder Drill:**

- **Linguistic & Theoretical Guidelines:**
  Fingerspelling in ISL is primarily two-handed. Your dominant active hand points, traces, or taps letters onto your non-dominant base hand. The base hand should be held flat and steady around chest level to act as a clear canvas.
- **Detailed Practice Steps:**
  - Step 1: Identify your dominant hand. Keep your non-dominant hand completely relaxed and stationary as the base.
  - Step 2: Practice forming consonants like 'B' (two hands side-by-side) and 'C' (curved fingers of dominant hand).
  - Step 3: Stabilize your wrists to prevent vertical bouncing while moving from letter to letter.
- **Covered Vocabulary:** Alphabets A-Z, Active hand, Base hand, Spell name
- **🎮 Duolingo Interactive Challenges:**
  1. **Multiple Choice Question:**
     * *Prompt:* What is the mechanical difference between ASL and ISL fingerspelling?

     * ❌ ISL does not have any vowels
     * ❌ ISL requires hands to be held above the head
  2. **Vocabulary Matching Pairs:**
     * `Vowels` ↔ `Pointing to fingertips`
